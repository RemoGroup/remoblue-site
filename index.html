Proposed managed setup

What a RemoBlue setup is designed to include

  • A RemoBlue workspace configured for the business.
  • A dedicated business number for voice and SMS, powered by Twilio.
  • A configured Mac mini for the proposed Apple messaging setup.
  • Connections to agreed email, calendar, and marketing accounts.
  • CRM and follow-up configuration.
  • Owner onboarding and agreed ongoing service.

Installation arrangements are confirmed during your intro call.
